👨‍🎓 About Me

I am an M.S. student (entering 2025) in Information and Communication Engineering at the University of Science and Technology of China, and received my B.S. in Data Science and Big Data Technology (Mathematics, top 10%) from Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen.

My research focuses on controllable and long multi-shot video generation and video/embodied world models—planning persistent entity states across cuts, keeping world states coherent under camera interventions, and translating predictive video pathways into robotic actions. I also work on efficient 3D perception and knowledge distillation.

As first author, I proposed CaT-Movie (with CaT-MBench) and WRBench. At X-Humanoid, I led camera-controlled video generation and conducted a controlled diagnostic study of a one-step Cosmos3-Edge world-action model. My publications include first-author papers at ICLR 2026 and MICCAI 2024 (top 11%), and collaborative work at EMNLP 2026 and in Medical Image Analysis 2025.

I am looking for a full-time internship in a core video, multimodal, or world-model foundation team (pre-training, RL post-training, or research) and welcome collaborations. View my CV (EN) / CV (CN) or email me.

CaT-Movie: Balancing Global Planning and Local Rendering for Long Multi-Shot Video Generation

J. Lu*, H. Shi*, Y. Guan, J. Xia, B. Wu, Y. Chen, Z. Xiong†.

  • Problem: Per-shot models forget entities across cuts, joint windows drift over boundaries, and similarity memory copies prior framing.
  • Approach: Decouples generation into a Global Storyboard Planner (Qwen3.8-27B + state compiler) and a 5B Local Window Renderer with shot-wise cross-attention, negative-RoPE entity-routed sparse attention, and anchor recall.
  • Result: Releases 358K-shot CaT-Movie-Data and cut-gated CaT-MBench, achieving 0.597 best average across 8 methods and surpassing 14B HoloCine (0.571) and StoryMem (0.527).

First Author 2026.06

Current World Models Lack a Persistent State Core

J. Lu, D. Zhu, H. Shi, L. Cai, G. Tang, Y. Chen, J. Cao, D. Tang, Y. Zhang, Y. Dai, X. Ju†.

  • Question: Is rendering decoupled from world-state evolution when camera motion changes only the observation?
  • Approach: WRBench separates camera execution, visual integrity, visible evolution, re-observation support, and conditional endpoint consistency across four native camera-control interfaces.
  • Finding: Across 9,600 videos from 23 models, viewpoint changes disrupted event evolution; better visuals and re-observation support did not reliably yield correct event endpoints.

Johnson-Lindenstrauss Lemma Guided Network for Efficient 3D Medical Segmentation

J. Lu, L. Cai, Y. Chen, G. Tang, S. Jiang, H. Shi, Z. Xiong†.

  • Task: Preserve 3D representation quality and fine detail under a limited computational budget.
